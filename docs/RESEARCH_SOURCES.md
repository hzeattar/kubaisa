# Research Sources & Brand Documentation

## 1. User Attached Imagery (Baseline)
- **Observations:** Displayed interior snapshots of furniture showrooms. Identified soft, rounded modern sofas in cream and off-white fabrics, as well as highly detailed gilded neo-classical pieces (champagne gold, carved wood).
- **Influence:** Guided the creation of the proxy furniture compositions in `ModernLiving.tsx` and `NeoClassicLiving.tsx`.

## 2. Qubaisa / Kubaisa Furniture (Facebook)
- **Observations:** Confirmed the brand identity (قبيصة للأثاث) and the mix of modern and neo-classical luxury offerings.
- **Influence:** Used to name the primary application and guide the architectural scale (flagship palace).

## 3. Hayah Modern & Kabassa Contemporary
- **Observations:** Contemporary lines, use of boucle fabrics, sleek metal accents.
- **Influence:** Modern living proxy data includes "Boucle Fabric" and "High-Density Foam" to reflect contemporary Egyptian luxury furniture trends.
